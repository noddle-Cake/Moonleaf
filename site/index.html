How it works

From a novel’s page to a finished book.

Moonleaf does the fetching and formatting on your phone. You don’t need a computer, a server, or an account.

  1. Add a novel

    Paste a link to a story on Royal Road, Scribble Hub, FanFiction.net, AO3, Standard Ebooks, RoliaScan or Indra Translations, or to any of its chapters. Moonleaf finds the title, author, cover and synopsis and puts it on your shelf.

  2. It downloads by itself

    Moonleaf starts downloading right away, collecting the chapters in order into a clean EPUB 3 book and tidying names to “Chapter 1: …”.

  3. Read, or take it anywhere

    The novel’s page lists every chapter, Mihon-style. Tap one to read, or Resume where you stopped. Check for new chapters any time, or save the EPUB for another e-reader.

The reader

A reader with real depth, made for long sessions.

Moonleaf’s reader follows KOReader’s approach: tap the sides to turn pages, the top for the menu, and the bottom for typesetting. Every setting changes the page live.

Pages or scrolling

Turn pages with taps, swipes or a keyboard, or switch to continuous scrolling. Pinch to resize text.

Seven page colors

Paper, Sepia, Sage, Dusk, Night, E-ink and E-ink dark, or follow the app’s light, dark or e-reader appearance.

A status bar you control

Show page numbers, pages and time left in the chapter or book, progress, the chapter title and a clock.

Find anything

Full-text search across the book, a skim slider, go to page or percent, and a quick way back after every jump.

Bookmarks and footnotes

Dog-eared bookmarks, contents with page numbers, and footnotes that preview in place instead of losing your page.

Reading statistics

Time read, words per minute, a 14-day history, and time-left estimates based on your own pace.

Read aloud

Listen with your phone’s voice or a free offline AI voice. The sentence is highlighted, pages turn on their own, and it keeps going with the screen off.

Hold to define

Hold any word to see what it means, from Wiktionary or a dictionary built into the app, and hear it read aloud.

Turn pages with buttons

Use the volume buttons, a page-turner remote, a keyboard or headset media keys, in whichever direction you like.

The library

A home for every story you follow.

Moonleaf’s library takes its cues from Mihon: covers in a grid or a compact list, shelves for Plan to read, Reading and Completed, search, and a history of what you opened last.

  • Progress bars on every cover, and Continue reading right where you stopped.
  • Open an EPUB file to add books you already own.
  • Light, dark and e-reader appearances for the whole app.
  • Export any book as a standard EPUB 3 file whenever you like.

Private by design

Your library stays on your phone.

Moonleaf has no accounts and no cloud. The app talks only to the novel sites you choose, and to Wiktionary when you look up a word.

  • No sign-up, no ads, no analytics or tracking.
  • Books, progress, highlights and statistics are stored on the device.
  • Chapters are fetched directly from the source website.
  • Read Aloud and the natural voice run on your phone; nothing you read is uploaded.
  • Look-ups happen only when you ask.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Moonleaf?

Moonleaf is a free Android app for reading web novels. It saves chapters from supported sites as an EPUB file on your phone and opens them in a built-in reader with KOReader-style typesetting, page colors, bookmarks, highlights and reading statistics. It can also open EPUB files you already have.

Is Moonleaf free?

Yes. Moonleaf is free, has no ads, and needs no account. The source code is on GitHub.

Which websites does Moonleaf support?

Moonleaf supports Royal Road, Scribble Hub, FanFiction.net and FictionPress, AO3, Standard Ebooks, RoliaScan and Indra Translations. Paste a story’s page or a link to any of its chapters, and Moonleaf downloads the whole book. For AO3 and Standard Ebooks it uses the site’s own EPUB. Indra Translations supports publicly readable chapters; premium or login-only chapters stop the download with an access message. Scribble Hub and FanFiction.net may ask you to complete a quick human check in the app. Wattpad is not supported because its terms prohibit downloading.

How do I install the Moonleaf APK on Android?

Download the APK from the download page, open the downloaded file, allow your browser to install apps when Android asks, then tap Install. Moonleaf needs Android 7.0 or later.

How do I update Moonleaf?

Open Settings → App Updates to compare versions and read release notes. Tap Download Update, then Install Update, and confirm in Android. You can cancel or retry downloads. Signed releases install over each other and keep your library, progress, highlights and settings. Builds 48 and earlier need one final reinstall to move to signed releases; save your EPUBs before uninstalling. Updates are installed manually.

Is Moonleaf on Google Play?

No. Moonleaf is distributed as an APK file published on GitHub with a SHA-256 checksum. Each update is a new APK download.

Can Moonleaf open EPUB files I already have?

Yes. Choose Open an EPUB File from the Library tab’s … menu or from Settings. The book is added to your library and opens in the reader.

Does Moonleaf work offline?

Reading works offline, including definitions (from a dictionary built into the app) and reading aloud. An internet connection is needed to fetch chapters from a website, to download the optional natural voice, and for fuller Wiktionary definitions and Wikipedia or translation look-ups.

Is Moonleaf related to KOReader or Mihon?

No. Moonleaf is an independent app. Its reader is inspired by KOReader and its library by Mihon, but it does not include code from either project and is not affiliated with them.

Does Moonleaf collect my data?

No. Moonleaf has no accounts, analytics or ads. Your library, reading positions, highlights and statistics are stored only on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not as a download yet. Moonleaf is built with Flutter and runs on iOS, macOS, Windows and Linux when built from the source code, but only the Android APK is published.
