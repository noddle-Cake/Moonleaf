Privacy policy

Short version: Moonleaf collects nothing about you. Last updated November 23, 2026.

The app

  • No accounts, ads or analytics. Moonleaf has no sign-in, contains no advertising or tracking libraries, and sends no usage data anywhere.
  • Your data stays on your device. Your library, shelves, saved EPUB files, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, reading statistics and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone. Uninstalling Moonleaf deletes them.
  • App updates. Opening Settings → App Updates requests version information from moonleaf.cjservin.com. Choosing Download Update downloads the APK from that same site. These requests do not include your library, reading history or account information. Updates are checked on request and installed only after Android asks you to confirm.
  • Network requests you start. When you add a novel or create an EPUB, Moonleaf connects directly to that website (such as Royal Road, RoliaScan or Indra Translations) to read its pages and cover image. Those sites see an ordinary request from your phone, as when you visit them in a browser.
  • Definitions. When you hold a single word, Moonleaf asks Wiktionary (run by the Wikimedia Foundation) for its definition, sending only that word. Without a connection it uses a dictionary built into the app instead. You can turn this off in the reader’s Controls settings.
  • Look-ups. If you choose Wikipedia, Translate or Open in Wiktionary, Moonleaf opens that service in your browser with the selected words. Nothing is sent unless you tap one of these.
  • Read Aloud. Books are read aloud by your phone’s text-to-speech or by Moonleaf’s natural voice, which runs entirely on your phone. If you choose to download the natural voice, it comes from GitHub. Your phone’s text-to-speech service is chosen in your phone’s settings and follows its own provider’s privacy terms.
  • Files you open. EPUB and font files you import are copied into the app’s private storage and are not uploaded anywhere.

This website

moonleaf.cjservin.com is a static site that sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or third-party scripts. It is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. The download link points to GitHub, which hosts the APK under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

Moonleaf does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions can be raised on GitHub.
