November 22, 2026

Update without reinstalling

  • Permanent signing key. Published APKs are now signed release builds. Future signed releases install over each other, keeping your books, progress, highlights and settings.
  • App Updates in Settings opens the download page. Download the APK, open it, and choose Update in Android.
  • Older debug builds need one final reinstall. Builds 48 and earlier used different signing keys. Save your EPUBs and read the migration instructions before uninstalling.

Read from Indra Translations

  • Indra Translations is now in Sources. Paste a novel page or chapter link, including Noble Lady Reformation Guide, to add it to your library.
  • Paragraphs keep the order shown on the site, with chapter formatting preserved and hidden filler left out.
  • Publicly readable chapters are supported. Premium or login-only chapters stop the download with an access message.

November 20, 2026

Listen to your books

  • Read Aloud. Tap the headphones in the reader’s menu and Moonleaf reads from the top of the page, sentence by sentence, on into the next chapters. The sentence being read is highlighted and pages turn on their own.
  • Keeps reading with the screen off. Pause, skip or stop from the notification. The player in the reader also steps back or forward a sentence and changes the speed, from 0.75× to 2×.
  • Two free voices. Use your phone’s own voice, or download Moonleaf’s natural voice once (42 MB): an AI voice that runs on your phone, works offline and has eight speakers to choose from.

Look up words

  • Hold a word to see its definition right above the menu. Tap it for every meaning with examples.
  • Works offline. Definitions come from Wiktionary, or from a dictionary built into the app when you’re offline.
  • Hear it. The speaker button reads the word and its definition aloud.
  • Turn the definitions off in Aa › Customize › Controls.

Fixes

  • Volume buttons turn pages again. After the notification prompt or sharing a link to Moonleaf, they changed the volume instead.
  • On e-ink screens, the buttons in the menu for selected text are readable again.

November 19, 2026

Start reading sooner

  • Read while it downloads. A book opens as soon as its first chapter arrives. New chapters appear in the open book as they download, without moving your place.
  • Pick up where you are. Add a book from a link to, say, chapter 21 and Moonleaf downloads chapter 21 first, then the chapters after it, then 1 to 20 last. The book opens at chapter 21 with the earlier chapters marked as read.
  • Downloads keep going in the background. They continue with the screen off or another app open, with a notification that shows progress and has a Cancel button. A download that stops, even when the app closes, resumes where it left off.
  • Faster checks for new chapters. Moonleaf remembers each book’s newest chapter and checks only that page for a Next link, instead of downloading the whole book again. Books downloaded with an older version do one full download the first time they update.

E-ink screens

  • No more flashing menus. A new E-ink Screen setting shows menus and settings instantly, with solid bars and no animation, so the screen doesn’t flash when you tap the middle of a page.
  • On automatically for Hisense, Onyx Boox, Kobo, PocketBook, Bigme, Meebook and other e-ink devices. Change it in Aa › Customize › Theme.

Getting started

  • A short welcome guide on first launch explains adding books and turning pages, with an E-ink Screen switch. Open it again from Settings › Welcome Guide.
  • Share to Moonleaf. In your browser, tap Share and choose Add to Moonleaf to add the story you’re reading.
  • A Paste button on the add screen fills in a link you copied.

November 18, 2026

Adding books

  • Paste any chapter link. A link to any chapter, not just the story’s main page, adds the whole book.
  • Downloads start right away. As soon as you add a link, Moonleaf downloads the book. There’s no separate “create EPUB” step.
  • New sources:
    • AO3 and Standard Ebooks: Moonleaf downloads the EPUB file the site already offers.
    • Scribble Hub and FanFiction.net: these sites use a Cloudflare human check, so Moonleaf loads them in a hidden in-app browser. If the check needs you, it appears on screen; tap through it and the download continues.
  • Tidier chapter names. Chapters read “Chapter 1: …”, and the book’s title is removed when it repeats inside a chapter name.

Library and book pages

  • Five tabs: Library, Updates, Reading, Settings and Search.
  • Updates shows downloads in progress, books with new chapters, and a button to check every book at once.
  • Reading has Continue Reading with your progress, then Recently Opened and Finished.
  • Book pages list every chapter, with a large Start Reading, Resume or Read Again button.
  • Quick actions: long-press a cover to Resume, Change Shelf, Check for New Chapters or Remove.

Reader

  • Turn pages with hardware buttons. The volume buttons can turn pages, with an option to swap their direction. You can also assign other buttons, such as a page-turner remote, a keyboard or headset media keys, to next and previous page.
  • Simpler menus. Tap the middle of the page to show them. The top has Close, Search and Bookmark; the bottom has Contents, a progress slider, Aa and a … menu.
  • Quick settings. Aa opens a sheet in the style of Apple Books for font size, page color, page-turn style and font. Customize… in that sheet leads to every detailed setting.
  • One place for your notes. Contents, Bookmarks and Highlights share one sheet that you switch between at the top.
  • Closer selection menu. The highlight and copy menu appears right next to the text you select.
  • Screen reader support. TalkBack can now read the pages aloud.

Look and feel

  • A cleaner design modeled on Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines, with matching icons, alerts, menus and loading spinners, and swipe-back navigation.
  • Fits slim phones down to 320 points wide, and works with very large text. At the largest sizes the library switches from covers to a list.
  • Respects your motion setting. Android’s “Remove animations” setting turns off page transitions, and the e-ink modes stay black and white.

Something not working? Background downloads, Share to Moonleaf and the e-ink mode are new, so tell us how they behave on your device by opening an issue on GitHub.

Older builds and their checksums are on the GitHub releases page. New to Moonleaf? Read the download and install guide.
