What’s new in Moonleaf

Released November 18, 2026. Install it over your current version: your library, reading positions and highlights carry over.

Download the APK

Adding books

  • Paste any chapter link. A link to any chapter, not just the story’s main page, adds the whole book.
  • Downloads start right away. As soon as you add a link, Moonleaf downloads the book. There’s no separate “create EPUB” step.
  • New sources:
    • AO3 and Standard Ebooks: Moonleaf downloads the EPUB file the site already offers.
    • Scribble Hub and FanFiction.net: these sites use a Cloudflare human check, so Moonleaf loads them in a hidden in-app browser. If the check needs you, it appears on screen; tap through it and the download continues.
  • Tidier chapter names. Chapters read “Chapter 1: …”, and the book’s title is removed when it repeats inside a chapter name.

Library and book pages

  • Five tabs: Library, Updates, Reading, Settings and Search.
  • Updates shows downloads in progress, books with new chapters, and a button to check every book at once.
  • Reading has Continue Reading with your progress, then Recently Opened and Finished.
  • Book pages list every chapter, with a large Start Reading, Resume or Read Again button.
  • Quick actions: long-press a cover to Resume, Change Shelf, Check for New Chapters or Remove.

Reader

  • Turn pages with hardware buttons. The volume buttons can turn pages, with an option to swap their direction. You can also assign other buttons, such as a page-turner remote, a keyboard or headset media keys, to next and previous page.
  • Simpler menus. Tap the middle of the page to show them. The top has Close, Search and Bookmark; the bottom has Contents, a progress slider, Aa and a … menu.
  • Quick settings. Aa opens a sheet in the style of Apple Books for font size, page color, page-turn style and font. Customize… in that sheet leads to every detailed setting.
  • One place for your notes. Contents, Bookmarks and Highlights share one sheet that you switch between at the top.
  • Closer selection menu. The highlight and copy menu appears right next to the text you select.
  • Screen reader support. TalkBack can now read the pages aloud.

Look and feel

  • A cleaner design modeled on Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines, with matching icons, alerts, menus and loading spinners, and swipe-back navigation.
  • Fits slim phones down to 320 points wide, and works with very large text. At the largest sizes the library switches from covers to a list.
  • Respects your motion setting. Android’s “Remove animations” setting turns off page transitions, and the e-ink modes stay black and white.

Something not working? Hardware-button page turns and the in-app human check are new, so tell us how they behave on your phone by opening an issue on GitHub.

Older builds and their checksums are on the GitHub releases page. New to Moonleaf? Read the download and install guide.
